*Last updated: August 2, 2026.*

This website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate traffic and improve the site. Depending on your browser and Google Analytics settings, this may include information such as pages viewed, referral source, device and browser type, and approximate location. See [Google's privacy information](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details.

Some links to this website include standard campaign parameters (such as `utm_source`) so I can distinguish visits from sources such as LinkedIn, X, GitHub, a CV, or an email signature. These parameters identify the referring link or campaign; this website does not use them to create a browser fingerprint.

Messages submitted through the contact form are processed by [Formspree](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy/) and delivered to me. The form asks for your name, email address, and message so I can respond. Please do not submit sensitive personal information.

For privacy questions or requests concerning information you submitted through this site, email [rodrigo.farinha@tuta.io](mailto:rodrigo.farinha@tuta.io).
